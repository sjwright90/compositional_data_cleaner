## Implement
- `CellStatus` StrEnum with all nine values from §4.1.
- Frozen containers: `RawLong`, `CleanLong`, `WideTable` (values/status/dl), `Metadata`, `ImputerInput`, `ImputedTable`.
- `WideTable.__post_init__` invariants: identical index/columns across frames; `ND` ⇔ `dl` notna and `values == dl`; `MISSING_*` ⇔ `values` isna.
- Derived views only: `nd_mask`, `missing_mask`, `ImputedTable.imputed_mask`; `WideTable.select()` returns a new object; `to_export(names)` renders `<number`.
- A helper counting observed cells for drop-plan use that counts only `DETECT` and `ND` (A5); `GT` is "observed" for display only.
- `core/contracts.py`: pandera schemas for raw long, clean long and wide boundaries.

## Done when
- Construction fails on misaligned frames, on `ND` with no DL, and on `MISSING_*` with a value.
- Masks are not stored attributes; mutating a source frame after construction cannot change the artifact (defensive copies or read-only).
- Observed-count helper excludes `GT`.
- `./scripts/check.ps1` passes.

## Out of scope
Stage logic, pivoting, Parquet persistence, the imputer.
